## TBL11: current PDX sample-slot capacity

Assessment block: Initial standard PDX target supports at most 96 distinct samples
Assessment code: slot_capacity

Saved bridge target_mapping has 127 chunks and 127 distinct sample_id values.
The projection resamples/encodes mixed active spans into target chunks of up
to 8 seconds; exact encoded-byte duplicates reuse one sample_id. Consequently
127 is the generated target sample count after that deduplication, not a count
of original SegaPCM ROM instruments or source Key-On events.

py/pcm_mdx.py:171 rejects len(analysis.samples) > 96. This is the currently
implemented single-bank projection limit, not a claim that SegaPCM hardware
or every possible PDX implementation is limited to 96 source sounds. The saved
bridge is already aligned to sample_multiplier 39. Blocking occurs before
successful MDX/PDX packaging. Longer timeout or disabling the song loop cannot
remove this slot-count guard. No bank expansion or other remedy implemented.

## TBOUR05 and TBOUR06: MDX track-offset capacity

TBOUR05 mdx_build.log:
  data inconsistency: MDX track 4 offset exceeds the maximum 0xfffe
TBOUR06 mdx_build.log:
  data inconsistency: MDX track 9 offset exceeds the maximum 0xfffe

Both assessments contain mdx_capacity_exceeded and the block reason:
  MDX capacity exceeded; generated MML and PDX are retained

This concerns the encoded MDX track start offset, not the VGM loop address,
PDX waveform byte size, a note's duration or a driver timer period. Maximum
reported offset is 0xfffe (65534). Logs provide the first rejected track number,
not its actual oversized offset; no failing byte offset was reconstructed.

The typed helper parses/compiles FM with mmlx, then builds the 16-track FM/PCM
MDX with soundlog. Its existing capacity failure is classified by
py/pcm_mdx.py:497-504 as pcm_projection_blocked. The status name therefore does
not mean SegaPCM decoding failed. The logs alone do not localize the offset
exception between FM compilation and final extended-MDX serialization; no
additional execution was performed to distinguish those substeps.

Saved bridge counts:
- TBOUR05: 34 chunks / 34 distinct sample IDs; aligned multiplier 62.
- TBOUR06: 30 chunks / 30 distinct sample IDs; aligned multiplier 29.

Neither exceeds the 96-sample guard. Native waveform, target mapping, final
alignment and PDX packing artifacts exist. Saved FM opm.mml sizes are 669762
and 178747 bytes respectively; these are text sizes, not measured compiled
MDX sizes. No claim is made that reducing PDX audio size would fix MDX offsets.

## Conclusions and decisions

The three remaining failures are explicit target capacity guards, independent
of the earlier song-loop validation errors and the 180-second conversion
budget. More wall-clock time alone cannot fix these guards. No code, bank
support, output compaction, alternate compiler integration or fallback was
implemented. Keep the default timeout at 180. Successful file generation is
separate from forthcoming user listening results.
